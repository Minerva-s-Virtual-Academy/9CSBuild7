How to Play & Learn

1. Connecting Devices: Click on any computer, switch, or node. It will glow blue. Then click another node to connect a cable between them!

2. Removing Cables: Click on an existing cable line to remove it, or use the Remove Cables button to start fresh.

3. Checking Your Work: Whenever you're ready, click Check My Network. You will get gentle feedback on what is correct and what needs tweaking.

4. Testing Packets: Use Simulate Data Packets to watch animated data move along your network cables!

No Stress: There are no timers, no points lost, and no rush. Take all the time you need!
